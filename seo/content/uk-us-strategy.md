# UK and US content strategy (draft, 17 Oct 2026)

**Goal:** win paying clients in the UK and US through content, not only Nigerian clients.
**Status:** plan only; waiting for Ufuoma's answers to the questions at the end.

## The honest constraints

1. **No UK or US address means no Google Maps ranking there.** We can't rank for "web designer London" or "web design near me" in the US, and making city pages for places we don't work would break the rank-ready rules (doorway pages). We compete on searches without a place in them: problem searches ("website not showing on Google"), niche searches ("website for model coach") and AI answers.
2. **Trust is the main barrier for a Nigeria-based agency.** The content has to show a real person, real work and safe payment, and say openly that Elevate is based in Nigeria.
3. **Content is slow on its own.** Expect 3 to 6 months before organic UK and US enquiries. The same pages must also work as proof in cold email, LinkedIn and Upwork from day one.

## Market facts (sources at the end)

- **UK small business website prices (2026, from web design companies' own blogs, so rough):**
  - freelancers about £1,100 to £4,600 (one analysis of 600+ quotes)
  - agencies about £2,500 to £10,000
  - London agencies up to £8,000 to £25,000
- **Our UK price:** £600 Starter, £1,100 Business, so we're priced below most UK freelancers.
- **AI search for local businesses:** BrightLocal's 2026 survey of US consumers is quoted as 58% (BrightLocal) or 45% (Local Falcon) having used AI to find a local business. Local Falcon found 83% of restaurants invisible in ChatGPT, versus 14% on Google. All of these are from vendors, so call them directional.
- **AI website builders:** Lovable and Bolt build JavaScript apps by default. Lovable says apps created from 13 May 2026 are server-rendered; older ones may still be invisible to many crawlers. That's a real problem we already fix (we fixed MR DGN Group's site, which had 0 readable words).

## Positioning

**"Websites built to be found on Google and ChatGPT, for small service businesses and personal brands."** The selling points:
- SEO and AI readability built in, which most UK and US budget builders skip
- prices below UK freelancers
- UK working hours overlap (Nigeria is 0 to 1 hour from the UK)
- real proof: Runway Refined by Alek (UK) and MR DGN (found through ChatGPT)
