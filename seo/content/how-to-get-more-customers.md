## Outline (plain language)

1. **The short answer:** customers already search Google, Maps and ChatGPT. Be easy to find there, and easy to trust when they find you.
2. **How Google decides who shows up:** relevance, distance and prominence, in Google's own words, explained with a Nigerian example.
3. **Step 1: a website that sells while you sleep.** One page per service, readable by Google and AI. The MR DGN "0 words" example.
4. **Step 2: your Google Business Profile.** Verified, complete, real photos, services matching your website.
5. **Step 3: the same details everywhere.** Name, phone, address and services matching on your site, Google and directories.
6. **Step 4: reviews.** Ask every customer and reply to every review. BrightLocal data, labelled US.
7. **Step 5: keep it fresh.** Website posts that answer real customer questions, plus GBP updates and photos. Honest about what posts do.
8. **Step 6: show up in ChatGPT.** The MR DGN story.
9. **What to avoid:** fake reviews, keywords stuffed into your name, bought links, anyone promising "#1 on Google".
10. **A 10-minute checklist** the reader can do today.
11. **How Elevate helps** (call to action: free check on WhatsApp), plus FAQs.
