### Mansa Luxe: Katampe Extension, Guzape, Gwarinpa

Mansa Luxe is **not in the top 100** for any of these from an Abuja location.

| Search | Volume | Page 1 is held by | Can Mansa Luxe win it? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| house for sale in katampe extension | 20 | PropertyPro, Nigeria Property Centre (6 of 10), Hutbay, PrivateProperty | **Not with a page.** Portals with hundreds of listings own it. Win it by **listing the property on those portals**, linked to the site. |
| land for sale in katampe extension | — | the same portals | Same answer |
| katampe extension (the area itself) | 8,100 | area guides (abujaproperties, privateproperty, faster.ng, cwlagos), **YouTube (#2, #6)**, Facebook | **Yes. This is the opening:** an area guide with first-hand facts, plus a YouTube video with an exact-match title. The "How much is land in Katampe?" box appears here. |
| house for sale in guzape | 50 | **guzapehomes.com, a one-area niche site, is #1**, then portals | Possible, but Guzape has fewer listings to stand on |
| house for sale in gwarinpa | 30 | portals only | Hardest |

Search Console agrees (12 months to 4 Nov): Mansa Luxe gets impressions for "katampe extension" at an average of #32,
and **six different URLs** split those impressions (a landing page, a guide, a family-home page, an investment page and two blog posts). Google
can't tell which one is *the* Katampe page. Consolidating them into one strong hub, with the other pages linking to it, is the first fix.

**Recommendation:** keep **Katampe Extension** as the main target, but aim at the *area* search (8,100/month)
with a guide and a video, rather than "house for sale" (20/month, owned by the portals). Guzape comes second.
Gwarinpa isn't worth a fight with Mansa Luxe's current listings.

## 4. Rob Hoffman, "Everything I'd Do If I Wanted To Rank #1 on Google in 48 Hours" (4 Nov 2026)

Breakdown in the SEO skills repo format (adopt / adapt / reject). Verify each item against `references/sources.md` before it goes into the skill.

| Claim | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pick a specific, high-intent search you can win; check who ranks, not just keyword difficulty | **Adopt** | Matches what the Abuja results show: small sites rank for city phrases |
| Long, specific searches convert better | **Adopt as an opinion** | Practitioner consensus; no Google source |
| "Best X", "alternatives" and "vs" pages are the most bottom-of-funnel | **Adapt** | For a local agency, "best SEO company in Abuja" pages are listicles by third parties; we earn a mention on them rather than writing our own "best of" list about ourselves |
| Publish on your site, YouTube and LinkedIn; publish LinkedIn articles on the company page | **Adapt** | YouTube already ranks on page 1 for "katampe extension", so the video half is backed by this data. LinkedIn is unverified for Nigeria. Content must be rewritten for each platform, never copied |
| Key takeaways at the top, comparison tables, an author profile, an FAQ of 5–8 questions | **Adopt** | Google's helpful-content and E-E-A-T guidance supports clear answers and visible authorship; tables help readers. Google says no special markup or "chunking" is needed for AI features |
| Information gain (first-party data, real interviews) is the biggest lever | **Adopt** | Our own Search Console data and MR DGN results are exactly this, and nobody else has them |
| CTA congruency: book a call for buying searches, a lead magnet for learning searches | **Adopt** | The AI visibility check is the lead magnet for the "why isn't my business on ChatGPT" cluster |
| About five CTAs per page, including floating ones | **Adapt** | Keep the existing header CTA and WhatsApp button, and add one in-copy CTA early and one at the end. Don't clutter mobile |
| Check rankings with a VPN or incognito search | **Adapt** | Use Search Console plus DataForSEO geolocated results; incognito searches are personalised by location |
| "Rank #1 in 48 hours" | **Reject as a promise** | The video itself says most searches don't work like that. We never promise positions (CONTEXT.md) |
| Parasite SEO (ranking on someone else's domain) | **Reject** | Google's site reputation abuse policy. Our own company LinkedIn page is not parasite SEO; renting space on other sites is |

## 5. Ufuoma's AI-visibility checklist, checked

| Statement | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| Submit the site to Search Console and submit sitemap.xml | Correct |
| Pages must be server-rendered so crawlers see the text | Correct, and especially for AI crawlers, which mostly don't run JavaScript. Elevate's Next.js site passes |
| Check indexing in Search Console, monitor for a month or more | Correct. Use the Pages report and URL Inspection |
| `site:yourdomain.com` shows the indexed pages | Partly. It shows a *sample*; Google says not to treat it as a full index count. Search Console's Pages report is the real number |
| Answer customers' questions on the site; add FAQs | Correct |
| Own local SEO with targeted wording | Correct, plus the Google Business Profile, reviews and listings |
| Correct H1s and headings | Correct |
| A short answer at the top so ChatGPT can extract it | Correct as good writing. Google says no special "AI chunking" is needed |
| Schema markup so AI can read the site | **Partly.** Google's "AI features and your website" page (May 2026) says no special schema is needed for AI features. Schema still helps rich results and must match the visible text. It's not the AI lever; readable text and being *mentioned* elsewhere are |
